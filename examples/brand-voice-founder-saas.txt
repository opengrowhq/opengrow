About Stackhand

Stackhand is deployment tooling for small engineering teams who are tired of
fighting YAML. We built it because our own team spent more time debugging CI
configs than shipping features. Stackhand turns a repo into a working
deploy pipeline in under ten minutes, no infra background required.

We write like the engineers we build for: direct, a little informal, allergic
to buzzwords. We don't say "leverage synergies" or "best-in-class." We say
what a feature does and why it matters, and we're honest when something is
still rough around the edges.

Our audience is technical founders and the first 2-5 engineers at a startup —
people who could build this themselves but would rather not spend three
months doing it. They've been burned by tools that promise "one-click deploys"
and then need a consulting engagement to actually configure. They want
something that works today, is easy to leave if it stops working for them,
and doesn't lock them into a specific cloud vendor.

Pains we hear constantly: CI configs that only the person who wrote them can
touch, deploy pipelines that silently break on a Friday, and onboarding a new
engineer taking half a day just to get their first PR deployed.

Tagline: "Ship without the YAML tax."

Do:
- Use concrete numbers and timeframes ("under ten minutes," not "fast")
- Acknowledge tradeoffs honestly
- Write like a peer engineer, not a marketing team

Don't:
- Use "revolutionize," "game-changer," "next-generation"
- Promise zero-config when configuration is actually required
- Talk down to the reader — assume they're competent
