# Commercial Use

OpenGrow is AGPL v3 open source. You can self-host it for free, inspect it, modify it, and use the full product without a crippled feature tier.

Hosted plans and commercial licenses exist for convenience, legal compatibility, support, and operations.

## What stays free

The core product stays free and self-hostable:

- Brand context ingestion.
- AI content generation.
- GitHub PR-based publishing.
- Analytics and revenue attribution.
- REST API access.
- Export/import paths.
- Self-hosted deployment.

## Hosted convenience

Hosted OpenGrow will sell operational convenience:

- Managed deployment.
- Managed upgrades.
- Managed backups.
- Managed worker capacity.
- Optional managed AI keys.
- BYOK support.
- OAuth setup for publishing and analytics integrations.
- Priority support.
- Uptime monitoring.

## Commercial license

Some companies cannot use AGPL software because of internal legal or procurement rules. Those companies can request a commercial license.

A commercial license is for:

- AGPL-incompatible use cases.
- Private deployment support.
- Enterprise procurement.
- Security review assistance.
- Support terms and upgrade guidance.

It is not a paywall for core self-hosted functionality.

## Hosted plans

Hosted OpenGrow will be offered in two plans:

| | Starter | Scale |
|---|---|---|
| Price (monthly) | $16/mo | $39/mo |
| Price (6-month) | $72 ($12/mo) | $174 ($29/mo) |
| Creatives per month | 200 | 650 |
| Concurrent generations | 2 | 6 |
| Models & features | Selected | All |
| Priority support | — | ✓ |

These limits apply to the hosted service only — they pay for managed
capacity. Self-hosted OpenGrow has none of them: the full product,
unlimited, free.

Team collaboration is included for free: invite members to your workspace and
work together on brands, content, and publishing — no per-seat charge in
either the self-hosted or the hosted product. (Seat-based billing shipped in
0.2.0 and was removed in 0.3.0; it is not offered.) Payments are processed by
our merchant of record, which handles global VAT and sales tax.

For commercial licensing, open a [GitHub Discussion](https://github.com/opengrowhq/opengrow/discussions).
